Kapitel 34: Bhaktiyogaḥ Bhagavad Gītā XII: Das Yoga der Hingabe

arjuna uvāca evaṃ satatayuktā ye bhaktās tvāṃ paryupāsate ye cāpy akṣaram avyaktaṃ teṣāṃ ke yogavittamāḥ
