Kāṭhaka-Saṃhitā · Aśvamedha-Abschnitt 4

Anuvāka 2 Svāhā-Spenden an alle Arten von Gewässern, von Brunnen bis zum Meer.

Pāda 162.5a

kūpyābhyas svāhā

Den Brunnenwassern svāhā!

Pāda 162.5b

kulyābhyas svāhā

Den Kanalwassern svāhā!

Pāda 162.5c

sthālyābhyas svāhā

Den Gefäßwassern (aus Töpfen) svāhā!

Pāda 162.5d

avaṭyābhyas svāhā

Den Grubenwassern svāhā!

Pāda 162.6e

khalyābhyas svāhā

Den Wassern der Vertiefungen svāhā!

Pāda 162.6f

hradyābhyas svāhā

Den Seewassern svāhā!

Pāda 162.6g

sūdyābhyas svāhā

Den Wassern der Sickerquellen [?] svāhā!

Pāda 162.6h

palvalyābhyas svāhā

Den Tümpelwassern svāhā!

Pāda 162.7i

vaiśantībhyas svāhā

Den Wassern der Teiche svāhā!

Pāda 162.7j

sarasyābhyas svāhā

Den Wassern der Seen svāhā!

Pāda 162.7k

varṣyābhyas svāhā

Den Regenwassern svāhā!

Pāda 162.7l

avarṣyābhyas svāhā

Den Nicht-Regenwassern svāhā!

Pāda 162.8m

pruṣṭābhyas svāhā

Den Tauwassern [?] svāhā!

Pāda 162.8n

hrādunibhyas svāhā

Den rauschenden Wassern svāhā!

Pāda 162.8o

sthāvarābhyas svāhā

Den stehenden Wassern svāhā!

Pāda 162.8p

syandamānābhyas svāhā

Den fließenden Wassern svāhā!

Pāda 162.9q

nādeyībhyas svāhā

Den Flusswassern svāhā!

Pāda 162.9r

saindhavībhyas svāhā

Den Wassern des Indus (der Ströme) svāhā!

Pāda 162.9s

samudriyābhyas svāhā

Den Meereswassern svāhā!

Pāda 162.10t

sarvābhyas svāhā ‖

Allen svāhā!
