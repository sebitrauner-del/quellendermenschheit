Mantra 24

ud agād ayam ādityo viśvena tapasā saha sapatnān mahyaṃ randhayan mā cāhaṃ dviṣate radhaṃ taved viṣṇo bahūdhā vīryāṇi tvaṃ naḥ pṛṇīhi paśubhir viśvarūpaiḥ sudhāyāṃ mā dhehi parame vyoman
