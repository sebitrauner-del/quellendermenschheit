tataś cainam anyābhyāṃ hastābhyāṃ prāśīr yābhyāṃ caitaṃ pūrva ṛṣayaḥ prāśnan brāhmaṇaṃ haniṣyasīty enam āha taṃ vā ahaṃ nārvāñcaṃ na parāñcaṃ na pratyañcam ṛtasya hastābhyām tābhyām enaṃ prāśiṣaṃ tābhyām enam ajīgamam eṣa vā odanaḥ sarvāṅgaḥ sarvaparuḥ sarvatanūḥ sarvāṅga eva sarvaparuḥ sarvatanūḥ saṃ bhavati ya evaṃ veda
