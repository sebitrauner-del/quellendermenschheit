Sūkta 107

Atharvaveda-Saṃhitā, Kāṇḍa 6

Mantra 1

viśvajit trāyamāṇāyai mā pari dehi trāyamāṇe dvipāc ca sarvaṃ no rakṣa catuṣpāḍ yac ca naḥ svam

Viśvajit (All-Erobernde), übergib mich der Trāyamāṇā (Schützerin); Trāyamāṇā, schütze alles, was uns eigen ist, Zweifüßler und Vierfüßler.
