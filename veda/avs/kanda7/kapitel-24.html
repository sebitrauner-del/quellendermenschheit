Atharvaveda-Saṃhitā · Kāṇḍa 7

Sūkta 24

Mantra 1

yan na indro akhanad yad agnir viśve devā maruto yat svarkāḥ tad asmabhyaṃ savitā satyadharmā prajāpatir anumatir ni yachāt

Was Indra für uns gegraben hat, was Agni, alle Götter, die Maruts, was die schön Singenden – das gewähre uns Savitṛ, der wahrhaft Rechtsame, Prajāpati, Anumati.
