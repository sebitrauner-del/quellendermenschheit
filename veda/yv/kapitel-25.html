Kaṇḍikā 1

śādaṃ dadbhiravakāṃ dantamūlairmṛdaṃ barsvaistegāndaṃṣṭrābhyāṃ sarasvatyā agrajihvaṃ jihvāyā utsādamavakrandena tālu vājaṃ hanubhyāmapa āsyena vṛṣaṇamāṇḍābhyāmādityāṃ śmaśrubhiḥ panthānaṃ bhrūbhyāṃ dyāvāpṛthivī vartobhyāṃ vidyutaṃ kanīnakābhyāṃ śuklāya svāhā kṛṣṇāya svāhā pāryāṇi pakṣmāṇyavāryā ikṣavo'vāryāṇi pakṣmāṇi pāryā ikṣavaḥ
