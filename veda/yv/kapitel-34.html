Vājasaneyi-Saṃhitā (Śukla-Yajurveda)

Kaṇḍikā 1

yajjāgrato dūramudaiti daivaṃ tadu suptasya tathaivaiti dūraṅgamaṃ jyotiṣāṃ jyotirekaṃ tanme manaḥ śivasaṅkalpamastu
