Bhāgavata-Purāṇa

Kapitel 89: caṇḍavegena purañjana-nṛpa-puram prati ākramaṇam, kāla-kanyāyāś ca caritram [?] Der Angriff Caṇḍavegas auf die Stadt des Königs Purañjana; das Wesen der Kālakanyā

Vers 1

nārada uvāca ittham purañjanaṁ sadhryag vaśam ānīya vibhramaiḥ purañjanī mahārāja reme ramayatī patim
