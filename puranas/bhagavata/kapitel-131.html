Bhāgavata-Purāṇa

Kapitel 131 Vṛtrāsuras ruhmvoller Tod

Vers 1

śrī-ṛṣir uvāca / evaṁ jihāsur nṛpa deham ājau / mṛtyuṁ varaṁ vijayān manyamānaḥ / śūlaṁ pragṛhyābhyapatat surendraṁ / yathā mahā-puruṣaṁ kaiṭabho 'psu
