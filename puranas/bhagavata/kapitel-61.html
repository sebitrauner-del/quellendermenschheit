Kapitel 61: sakāma-karma-bandhanam Verstrickung durch begierdegeleitetes Handeln

atha yo gṛha-medhīyān dharmān evāvasan gṛhe kāmam arthaṁ ca dharmān svān dogdhi bhūyaḥ piparti tān
