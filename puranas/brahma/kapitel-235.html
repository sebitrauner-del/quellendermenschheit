Kapitel 235: yogavarṇanam Die Darlegung des Yoga

Brahma-Purāṇa

idānīṃ brūhi yogaṃ ca duḥkhasaṃyogabheṣajam / yaṃ viditvāvyayaṃ tatra yuñjāmaḥ puruṣottamam //
